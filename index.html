01 — About

About

I'm a graduate student in Computer Science at the University of Chicago, with research interests spanning machine learning, natural language processing, computational biology, and human-computer interaction. I'm currently a member of the Human Computer Integration Lab, where I work on how computational systems can integrate with the body rather than simply observe it.

Before UChicago, I completed my BS in Computer Science at the University of South Florida, where I worked on ML for computational biology and built and deployed production NLP systems. I'm building toward a PhD, with interests that include BCIs and neurotechnology alongside broader questions in applied machine learning.

05 — Projects

Selected Work

TemporalBertSpam
PyTorch · DistilBERT · DeBERTa-v3 · PostgreSQL
A two-stage architecture combining transformer text encoding with a causal Transformer over sender message histories, deployed in production on 43,741 real outbound SMS messages with microsecond-precision carrier delivery timestamps. Achieves F1 0.838 and recall 0.913 on a 20,000-message production evaluation set.
GitHub
Algorithmic Trading Backtester
Python · XGBoost · Streamlit
A production backtesting framework for three quantitative strategies across 20 S&P 500 stocks (2015–2024), with structural lookahead-bias prevention and a 75-test validation suite. Momentum strategy achieved a Sharpe ratio of 1.30 with 30% annualized alpha over SPY.
